# Alice in Borderland

Imagine waking up in a city where all the people are gone...  
No work. No school.  
But to survive, you must play games — and losing means death.

That’s the world of **Alice in Borderland**, my favorite series.

---

## What’s It About?

Arisu, a smart but lost young man, finds himself in a strange Tokyo.  
To stay alive, he and others must win games — each card represents a level.

He meets strong and brave friends like **Usagi**, **Chishiya**, and **Kuina**,  
and together they fight to stay alive and find the truth behind Borderland.
